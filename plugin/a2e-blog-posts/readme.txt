Show your latest blog posts as a designed section on the homepage and on any page you choose, from the categories you choose. Also fixes the text alignment of the blog's category buttons.

== Description ==

* **Homepage blog section, ready on activation.** Your three newest guides appear near the bottom of the homepage, just before the final "free store audit" call to action, using the A2E Prime theme's own heading, button and post cards, so they match the site in light and dark mode.
* **Any page, any category.** Add as many blog sections as you like. For each one choose where it shows (homepage, every page, every service guide, every blog post, category pages, the blog page, or specific pages from a searchable list), where on the page (before the final call to action, above the footer, or before the FAQ), which categories, how many posts (1 to 12), the order (newest, most commented, random, oldest), 2, 3 or 4 columns, the heading, intro and button, and a soft band or plain background.
* **Related posts that fit.** On a blog post or category page a section can show posts from that same category (topped up with other recent posts when the category is small). A blog post never lists itself, and blog and category pages never repeat the posts already listed on them.
* **No double posts.** While a section is on the homepage, the theme's own homepage blog block is hidden. Your theme setting is not changed: switch the section off and the theme's block comes back.
* **Shortcode.** Place posts inside any page or post with [a2e_blog_posts], for example [a2e_blog_posts category="ebay" count="6"] or [a2e_blog_posts section="1"].
* **Category button fix.** On the blog page the category buttons (All, eBay, Amazon to eBay Dropshipping...) had their text stuck to the top. It is now centred. You can switch this off.

Nothing in the theme is edited. With other themes the sections use a simple built-in card design.

== Installation ==

1. Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, choose a2e-blog-posts.zip, Install, Activate.
2. Your homepage now shows your latest guides near the bottom.
3. To change it or add more sections: A2E > Blog Posts (or Settings > Blog Posts with other themes).

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Where do I edit the sections? =

A2E > Blog Posts. Each section is a card: tick where it shows, pick categories and options, then Save changes. "View it on the site" opens a page where it shows.

= How do I find a category's slug for the shortcode? =

The slugs are listed at the bottom of the A2E > Blog Posts screen.
